## MORE THAN JUST A SERVER.

Some servers are just somewhere to play.

Miners Online is different, its a place where we experiment with new ideas, explore new ways to play, and create experiences
that are designed around the things that make Minecraft worth coming back to.

## YOUR STORY STARTS HERE.

:::: info Nations

Nations is a survival game mode where players can claim land, build their own cities, and participate in a player-driven economy.

Players can also engage in politics and form governments.

Supports 1.8-26.2 on Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

::: warning In development
Most functionality is still in development and is not yet available to players.
:::

<VPButton
theme="brand"
text="Explore Nations"
href="./play/nations/"
/>

**Features**

1. Land claiming and protection
2. Player-driven economy
3. Shops and trading
4. Government and politics
::::

::: info Construct
Construct is our game mode focused around technical constructions and creative freedom.

This uses our light Fabric modpack focusing on automation and computing.

Supports only 26.2 on Java Edition.
